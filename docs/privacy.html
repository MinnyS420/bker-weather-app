Privacy policy

Moto Weather Guard · Last updated 4 September 2026

This page describes how the Moto Weather Guard Android app handles information. It is written to match what the app actually does. It is not legal advice.

Who we are

Moto Weather Guard is a standalone weather app for motorbike riders. There is no account, no backend server of ours, and no advertising.

Location

If you allow location access, the app reads the phone’s GPS position so the forecast matches where you are. That position is not stored on a server we run.

To fetch the forecast, the app sends latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo, a third-party weather service. Open-Meteo’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that request.

If you refuse location permission, the app falls back to the last known city or to Skopje. Location is optional for the app to open.

Notifications

Daily ride alerts are scheduled on the phone with the Android notification system. A notification is only scheduled when tomorrow’s verdict is yellow (warning) or red (danger). Those alerts stay on the device. We do not send them from a cloud service.

What we do not collect

  • No account or email inside the app
  • No contacts, photos, files, or payment data
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No sale of personal data

Settings stored on the phone

Language and reminder time are saved in local storage on the device so they survive an app restart. They are not uploaded to us.

Children

The app is a motorbike weather tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If how the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
